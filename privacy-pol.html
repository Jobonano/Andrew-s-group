01 Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to andrew-s-group.com (the "Site"), operated by Andrew's Group. It covers the Site's marketing pages, the AI chat assistant, and the contact form. Data handled as part of a specific client engagement — for example, systems built to process a client's own customer data — is instead governed by that engagement's separate agreement.

02 What data is collected

The Site is designed to collect the minimum data needed to respond to you. Depending on how you interact with it:

FeatureData collectedWhy
Contact formName, work email, company, service interest, and your messageTo respond to your inquiry and follow up about a consultation
AI chat widgetMessage text, a randomly generated session ID, and message history for that sessionTo generate a relevant reply and maintain conversational context
General browsingStandard technical data (browser type, approximate location from IP, pages visited)Basic site analytics and security

No account creation, passwords, or payment details are collected through this Site.

03 How data is used

  • To respond to messages sent via the contact form or chat widget.
  • To generate automated replies in the AI chat assistant based on your message and the ongoing conversation.
  • To schedule and prepare for a free consultation, where requested.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the Site.

Contact form and chat messages are not used to build an advertising profile of you, and are not sold to third parties.

04 Third-party services & processors

To provide the automated features on this Site, messages you send are processed by the following categories of third-party infrastructure:

Webhook / automation platform (n8n)
Language model providers
CRM / database storage

Each of these processes data under its own terms and security practices. Andrew's Group selects processors with reasonable care but does not control their independent security decisions.

05 Cookies & local storage

The Site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The AI chat widget keeps a temporary session identifier in memory for the duration of your visit so it can maintain conversation context; this identifier is not linked to your identity and is not persisted after you close the tab, beyond what is retained by the automation backend as described above.

06 Data retention

Contact form submissions and chat messages are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for a reasonable follow-up period afterward, or as required for legitimate business record-keeping. You can request earlier deletion at any time using the contact details below.

07 Data sharing & disclosure

Data is not sold. It may be shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, strictly to operate the features you use, or where disclosure is required by law.

08 Client project data

Where Andrew's Group builds an automation system for a client — for example, a lead-qualification workflow or an HR automation — that system may process the client's own business data (such as customer inquiries or employee records). The handling of that data is governed by the separate agreement for that engagement, not by this general Site policy, and Andrew's Group acts according to the instructions and access controls agreed with that client.

09 Your rights

You can request to:

  • Know what data has been collected about you through the Site's contact form or chat widget.
  • Request correction or deletion of that data.
  • Opt out of future contact.

To exercise any of these, reach out using the contact details below.

10 Children's privacy

This Site is directed at businesses and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Site's features change. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
