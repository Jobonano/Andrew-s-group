Beyond individual projects

I founded Andrew's Vantage.

Where Vision Meets Automation.

Andrew's Vantage is the AI automation and cloud infrastructure company I founded to take the kind of systems I build individually — AI agents, workflow automation, and reliable ML — and deliver them to businesses as a managed service. Where my personal projects are proofs of concept, Andrew's Group is where they become production systems for clients: audited, deployed, monitored, and supported after launch.

The company focuses on five areas: automating repetitive HR and people-ops work, building AI-driven lead generation pipelines, running social media content and posting on autopilot, providing the cloud infrastructure that keeps those systems online, and layering enterprise-grade protection around all of it. Every engagement follows the same process I use in my own work — audit, design, deploy, then optimize — so clients get a working system, not just a proposal.
